A little about me

A thoughtful mind.
An artist’s eye.

I’m Sofiia Sanzharovska, a multidisciplinary product and communication designer based in Kingston, Ontario. I work across digital experiences, visual identity, and publication design, with a particular interest in public services and culture.

My design background brings together a foundation in art with practical research and production. Painting, photography, and hands-on making continue to inform how I think about composition, colour, and storytelling.
